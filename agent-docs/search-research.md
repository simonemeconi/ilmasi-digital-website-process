# Search research

Search research conventions for ILMASI Digital web projects. Apply during workflow step 2 (Search Research), between discovery and copywriting.

## When to use

Load this doc after the discovery brief is approved and before the page structure is finalised.

## Output

Three things, in order of value:

1. **Which pages should exist** — gaps and redundancies in the planned structure.
2. **The intent each page serves** — one page, one job.
3. **The vocabulary the market uses** — how buyers name the problem, the service, themselves.

**Not a keyword list to hit.** Do not hand the copywriter phrases to insert.

## Start from the brief

- Discovery phase 7 keywords and phase 4 competitors are **hypotheses. Check every one.**
- Clients name the terms their industry uses internally. Buyers search for the problem, in their own words.

## Research in Italian, natively

- **Never translate a keyword list.** The Italian form of an intent is not a translation of the English one.
- Check **regional variation** — the same service is named differently across Italy.
- Cover both registers. The official name of a service and the phrase people use for it are often unrelated strings.

See [language.md](language.md).

## Local intent

- Distinguish searches carrying an explicit place from searches where location is implied.
- Decide: pages per area, or one page naming the areas covered.
- **Do not build near-identical pages differing only by place name.**
- Address, hours and service area go in `discovery/controller.md` and the brief.

## Competitors

- For each named competitor: which pages they have that the client does not, and what each is for.
- Look for pages everyone has and nobody needs.

## Method

- Work at the level of **intent groups**, not individual strings.
- One intent, one page. Where two overlap, decide which page owns which and record it.
- **Never invent volume figures or trends.** Unknown is an open question, not a gap to fill.
- Where evidence is thin, say so and let the decision be made on business grounds.

## Output location

Write findings into `discovery/brief.md`, in the page structure and target keyword sections. **Do not create a parallel document.**

- Page inventory, with additions and removals argued.
- One line per page: the intent it serves.
- Vocabulary grouped by intent.
- Open questions where evidence was thin.

## Handover

Copy owns the words, including titles and meta descriptions — see [copywriting.md](copywriting.md). Step 13 implements them. **Nothing here licenses re-deciding a page's focus at step 13.**
